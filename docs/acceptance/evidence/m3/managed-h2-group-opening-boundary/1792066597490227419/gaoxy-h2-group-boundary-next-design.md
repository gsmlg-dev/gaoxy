## All failure paths

1. Bad selected/Neutral/checked/identity/profile/limits: pure error, no retained diagnostics, no new open. Strip opening payloads; retain obtained group/transport tokens/pins for normal release; passive health cancelled/neutral.
2. Validation/pin/checkout denial before checked client: existing release/rollback order; strip any selected config/Neutral from the group cleanup state, no alternate selection.
3. Cancellation, owner/instance/consumer DOWN or opening/total deadline while waiting/prepared: clear source/options/K and URL; preserve tokens/pins; never notify ready.
4. Ordinary client.open error: drop prepared options/K and URL, feed existing normalized upstream failure mapping. Do not create a handle/completion. Attempted upstream failures retain existing health semantics; local errors remain neutral.
5. Cleanup-unconfirmed open result: preserve existing negative evidence and hold behavior. No fake completion, refund, fallback or success; full source/config/K is not needed to await owner-held completion. Follow existing transport_selection-specific stop/fail branch without losing token identity.
6. Transport bind error: original request/completion retained in cleanup state; group bind not attempted. Drop URL/options/K. Never ready/upload.
7. Group bind error after transport bind: original completion and both tokens remain; cleanup abort/join then finish transport/pins/group/Service; never ready/upload.
8. Cleanup pending/failure/deadline: remain fail closed with original public completion and finite reasons. Do not erase selection/pin to simplify a projection. No source/config/K remains in returned cleanup state.
